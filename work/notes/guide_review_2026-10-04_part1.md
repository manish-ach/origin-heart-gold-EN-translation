# Guide review, part 1: Pallet Town through Celadon

Date: 2026-10-04. Scope: all 1,326 lines of guide chapters 01–03, read for accuracy, clarity, repetition and useful cross-references. Review only; no game, translation, tool or guide edits.

## Method and limits

Consequential findings below were checked using `work/tools/docs/scriptdump.py` / `romdata.py`, which read `work/rom/origin_v4.0.3_cn.nds`, with current English banks for message labels. Script references are file numbers in `a/0/1/2`; `@` means byte offset, not document line. I also scanned every decoded script for writes to flags 1046, 1047, 106 and 2311. This is static verification, not an emulator playthrough. I did not independently rebuild collision/pathfinding evidence or verify every trainer party, shop, coordinate or engine opcode. Existing `guide_errata.md` supplied leads, not proof. Many of its chapter 1–2 corrections remain unapplied despite its status banner; chapter 3 has substantially more incorporated corrections.

## Priority corrections

1. **Pallet gifts: Daisy alone does not unlock them.** High confidence; blocks early rewards. `guide/01-pallet-to-pewter.md:33` says to talk to Daisy first, but file 736 @452–456 only reaches the gift unlock @1787 if Mom's Running Shoes flag 767 is already clear. File 842 @6123 clears 767; the savings responses @8811/@8830 check Town Map flag 408 before clearing gift flag 768 @9563. Bulbasaur has an earlier clear @7429. Replace with: **“After choosing your starter, talk to Mom for the Running Shoes, then get Daisy's Town Map. Collect your gift before entering Viridian.”** If explaining the reverse order, add only “If you saw Daisy first, finish Mom's savings conversation.” The exact unavoidable Viridian trigger geometry is a separate previously documented finding, not freshly collision-verified here.

2. **Misty's confession is assigned to the wrong side of the ending.** High confidence; following the current instruction can lose the opportunity. `guide/02-pewter-to-vermilion.md:319` says after the final Hall of Fame. File 758 @3665→6418→7044 instead requires flag 2311 **clear**. The final Hall of Fame sets it @822:2454. File 34 clears it @3187/@6152; subsequent League scripts also clear it. Replace with: **“Confess after the Radio Tower liberation and before the final Hall of Fame, with the love reading unlocked and Misty's route still open.”** Keep the male-player condition and link to chapter 9's full romance entry. Verify precise story access in a playthrough before asserting every point in that interval is reachable.

3. **Swablu does not require obtaining both starter-dependent gifts.** High confidence; newly re-derived error in chapter 3. `guide/03-vermilion-to-celadon.md:236` requires “both saved Pokémon (Bulbasaur and Growlithe).” File 9 initially sets both flags 1046/1047 @5538/@5542. A Route 5 victory clears only the relevant one (179 @2462 or @2828); accepting that gift sets it again (183 @232 or @420). Thus file 197 @1627–1642 checking both flags does **not** mean taking both Pokémon. Replace with: **“Win the Route 5 raid and accept the rescued Pokémon from the shelter: Bulbasaur for Pikachu starters, Growlithe otherwise.”** The unused counterpart is already marked complete.

4. **Yellow's second date has no healing between battles.** High confidence; material preparation error. `guide/01-pallet-to-pewter.md:356` promises a heal between four Multi Battles. File 739 heals @10519, battles @11662/@11800/@11963/@12101, then heals @12969. Replace with **“Four consecutive Multi Battles; your party is healed before and after the series.”**

5. **Moving in is presented as a working reward despite a script blocker.** High static confidence, runtime not demonstrated. `guide/01-pallet-to-pewter.md:361` promises the house sequence. File 843 @363–367 tests flag 106 and jumps to End @4292 before the partner branches. The starter sets 106 (738 @3946/@4080), and an all-script scan found no `ClearFlag 106`. Replace the instructions with **“Moving in appears blocked in the original hack; see Known issues.”** Retain the detailed intended Mail mechanics only in technical notes. Chapter 9 already acknowledges this blocker; chapter 1 must not contradict it. Do not claim absence of non-script engine mutation is proven.

6. **Route 2 warehouse gift has an omitted cutoff and incorrect battle trigger.** High confidence. `guide/01-pallet-to-pewter.md:188` says the Ariana fight happens on entry and recommends doing it first. File 171's grunt conversation offers the battle choice @114/@146; gift script 3 @238 onwards has only choice/party/starter checks. The Celadon finale sets the gift hide flag 1473 and warehouse flag 308 (853 @3782/@3786). Replace with **“The ball can be taken before or after the grunt/Ariana battle. Collect it before the Celadon Rocket Hideout finale empties the warehouse.”** Link directly to that finale, and distinguish the NPC interaction from entering the building.

7. **Cerulean burglary deadline remains an unresolved-sounding warning despite a definite script cutoff.** High confidence. `guide/02-pewter-to-vermilion.md:233` says the grunt “may disappear (not traced).” The Sabrina takeover sets case-closed flag 1024 and grunt hide flag 1033 (17 @12111/@12115). Cerulean's preconditions block reopening the lead; the normal lead clears 1033 @756:2131. Replace with **“Finish the burglary case before making Sabrina laugh in Pokémon Tower.”** Link to the Sabrina entry. The safest concise advice completes both the grunt and the reward visits before the deadline; an optional technical note can distinguish the surviving TM Case exchange from lost officer berries.

8. **Route 5 summary omits its deadline and contradicts the traced continuation.** High confidence. `guide/02-pewter-to-vermilion.md:191` and its follow-up need alignment with chapter 3. File 17 @12165 onwards closes an unfinished raid; file 197 and 183 distinguish the Swablu reward branches. Replace the follow-up with **“Finish the raid before making Sabrina laugh. After accepting the rescued Pokémon, continue the Swablu quest on Route 11: return it for 3 PP Max, or let the girl keep it for 1 PP Max and 2 Chesto Berries.”** Remove “the stolen-Pokémon branch also continues”; chapter 3 explicitly corrects that assertion. Use one canonical detailed Route 11 entry instead of duplicating the whole sequence.

9. **Surge's gift is wrong for Pikachu starters, in both chapters.** High confidence. `guide/02-pewter-to-vermilion.md:362` and chapter 3's shared-gifts sentence promise Light Ball universally. File 776 @1351 checks Pikachu starter flag 1288; normal branch gives item 236, alternate @1462 gives item 83 (Thunder Stone). Replace with **“Lt. Surge: Thunder Stone for Pikachu starters; Light Ball otherwise.”** Chapter 2 also says his line varies by side while chapter 3 correctly explains why it does not; remove that sentence. Add the townsfolk-only 2 Big Mushrooms already documented in chapter 3, or link to its single reward table.

10. **Nugget Bridge grunt is not a must-win retry.** High confidence. `guide/02-pewter-to-vermilion.md:263` says “you must win.” File 216 sets hide flag 1021 @1434 before battle @1438; loss whites out @5000 without undoing it. Misty's TM03 scene also sets 1021 (758 @3875). Replace with **“Refuse for a one-time battle; losing whites you out and he is gone. Meet him before receiving Misty's TM03.”** Add **“Under $10,000, accepting costs nothing”** if retaining the theft table: the money tests @4846–4965 fall through to disappearance with no deduction.
