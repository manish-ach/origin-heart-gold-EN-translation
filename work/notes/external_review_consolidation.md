## New check classes we should adopt

1. **Retained-US-line audit** (the most productive class). For every `origin: us` or `tm_v3` string, compare the hack Chinese with the vanilla Chinese/Japanese slot the US line came from, and list the lines where the hack rewrote the Chinese. Read those bilingually. This found most of the valid items: phone, radio, Frontier, Union Room, survey, Pokédex and battle banks.
2. **Identical-Chinese consistency.** Group all strings by normalised Chinese (tags other than layout kept, breaks removed) and flag groups with more than one English rendering. Our rerun found 439 groups, most harmless drift. Triage by bank family and fix the meaning splits (RB-D01/D02/D07/D08). Make the tutor, move-forget and nickname boilerplate uniform (P2).
3. **Prefix-break lint in `qa.py`** (Mt./Prof./S.S./Lt./Mr./Dr. followed by a break). AGENTS.md states the rule, but QA does not enforce it, and 33 strings break it now. Also add **mid-sentence `{SCROLL}`**, **`Lv.` split from its number** and **one-word `{CLEAR}` orphan** lints (RV-018; about 290 rough candidates).
4. **Worst-case overflow triage.** Drive the `line_may_overflow` warnings (17 on vanilla_us) and the 2 hack_v4 `line_too_wide` errors to zero, or give each a documented `qa_ignore`.
5. **Snapshot and drift log for reviews.** Freeze a snapshot before any review and diff it before applying the results. This caught a regression (LD-001) introduced by the menu-width follow-up.
6. **Register lint.** Check that every `refs` entry resolves to an existing bank and id (26 are broken now, from the PROGRESS migration where "0322/0326" became `0322#0326`). Also check scope/alias contradictions (RV-011) and stale decision texts that no longer match the bank (D-0902, D-0556, D-1288).
7. **Species and move identity lint beyond the glossary.** Old hack names such as 水流喷射 (Aqua Jet), 独角蛛 (Ariados), 臭臭泥 (Muk) and 羊咩咩 (Flaaffy) were missed where the English had a different species or move. QA should flag an English species or move name that differs from the glossary for the zh name present in the same string.
8. **Empty-target, tag-semantics and source re-decode checks** found nothing new. Our QA and D-1300 already cover them, so there is no need to adopt them.
