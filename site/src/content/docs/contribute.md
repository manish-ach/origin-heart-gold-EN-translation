This guide is maintained in the open. You don't need to know any code to help.

## Found a mistake?

Every page has these links at the bottom:

- **Report a problem with this page** opens a short form. Say what you expected and what happened in game.
- **Send a screenshot** opens a form where you can drag and drop screenshots, for example of a place the guide describes badly.
- **Suggest an edit** (quest guide pages only) opens the page's text on GitHub. Change it and submit your change for review.

Each quest on the quest guide pages also has its own **Report a problem with this quest** link, next to its **Done** tick box.

All of these go through GitHub, so you need a free GitHub account.

## What helps most

- **Confirming steps marked "not confirmed in game".** Most entries were read from the game's data and never played through.
  If you did it, tell us whether it worked.
- **Clearer directions.** "Three steps right of the Pokémon Center" beats any coordinate. If you got lost following a quest, tell us where.
- **Screenshots** of hard-to-find spots, puzzles and menus.

## How the site is made

The quest guide is written by hand. The Pokédex, locations, items, moves, trainers and move tutor pages are produced automatically
from the game's data, so they can't be edited directly. Report the problem on the page and we'll fix it at the source.
