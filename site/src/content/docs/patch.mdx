## Good to know

- **Nothing is uploaded.** Everything happens in your browser tab. Your file never leaves your device; the page only downloads the patch from this website.
- **Keep your original file.** The patcher makes a new file and never changes yours. You need the original again for every new version of the patch.
- **Checked twice.** The page only patches a clean HeartGold (USA) dump, CRC32 **C180A0E9**, and checks the finished game against the SHA-1 published with the release before it lets you save it.
- **Then play** the saved `.nds` in an emulator. The patch is tested in [melonDS](https://melonds.kuribo64.net/); other emulators haven't been tested. Playing on a real DS, DSi or 3DS? See [the FAQ](../faq/#can-i-play-on-a-real-ds-dsi-or-3ds).
  More help is on the [How to use this guide](../about/#what-you-need-to-play) page.

## What's new in rc8

Version **v1.0.0-rc8** (4.0.3-RC8 inside the game) is a release candidate. It is not yet tested on real hardware. Earlier releases are on the [changelog](../changelog/); the full technical list is in the changelog on <a href={REPO_URL}>GitHub</a>.

- **Freezes fixed:** stepping under the arch in front of the stairs on Victory Road (reported on a real DS Lite), the weekday siblings' ribbon scene, the Pallet Town photographer when none of your Pokémon can fight, losing to Giovanni at the Mt. Silver hot spring, and scripted Double Battles when you have fewer than two Pokémon (such as the Department Store's 6F).
- **Money and quantities:** the Goldenrod fortune teller, the younger Haircut Brother and the S.S. Anne shop now check for the price they charge; the Game Corner's "500 coins" option gives 500 coins. Rewards of several items say how many you got ("Obtained Ultra Ball x2!").
- **Story fixes:** the Ilex Forest bug hunt can no longer set off the Forest of Time scenes early, the Cynthia romance scenes take the Snow Mail they ask for, Petilil holding a Black Belt evolves straight into Hisuian Lilligant, the Blackthorn tutor teaches Play Rough and the Saffron dojo tutor always teaches Volt Switch. The Ice Stone and the Dragon Scale can be used from the Bag again, so the Pokémon that evolve with them can evolve.
- **Names and sprites:** the player's name no longer shows up blank in dozens of lines, and 36 stationary overworld Pokémon are drawn as the species the text and cry name.
- **Battles:** fainting Pokémon cry again and moves that deal damage play the hit sound.
- **Difficulty settings:** ITEMS LIMITED is renamed **DEFAULT** and now follows the original hack's battle-item rules (Potions included); **ALL** still adds twenty more medicines. SHARE OFF no longer disables a held Exp. Share, and CAP, SHARE and ITEMS now also apply in story battles you can lose. See [Difficulty settings](../difficulty/).
- **Text fixes:** the Route 21 Trainer's Lairon, the Celadon Skitty boy's "Fluffy Tail" and the Rock Tunnel tutor (who now names Pollen Puff, the move he teaches).

Fixes marked "Fixed in rc8" on the [Known issues](../guide/known-issues/) page are the ones above.

<details id="manual">
<summary>If the patcher doesn't work for you: patch by hand</summary>

You can do the same thing with any xdelta patcher:

1. Download the `.xdelta` patch from the <a href={REPO_URL + '/releases'}>newest release on GitHub</a>. Its `README.txt` lists the SHA-1 your patched game should have.
2. If your HeartGold file is zipped, unzip it first.
3. Open [xdelta-wasm](https://kotcrab.github.io/xdelta-wasm/), another in-browser patcher. Choose your HeartGold `.nds` as the source file and the `.xdelta` as the patch, apply it and save the new `.nds`.
4. Or use `xdelta3` from a terminal (replace the patch name with the file you downloaded):

   ```sh
   xdelta3 -d -s "Pokemon - HeartGold Version (USA).nds" Origin_HeartGold_v4.0.3_EN_<version>.xdelta Origin_HeartGold_EN.nds
   ```

</details>

## How it works

The page computes your file's CRC32 and SHA-1 (unpacking a `.zip` first with your browser's built-in decompression),
downloads the patch published with this website and applies it with
[xdelta3](https://github.com/jmacd/xdelta) compiled to WebAssembly by [xdelta-wasm](https://github.com/kotcrab/xdelta-wasm),
inside a background worker in your browser tab. Both are open source under the Apache License 2.0; see the [credits](../about/#credits).
